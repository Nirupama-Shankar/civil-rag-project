SLUMP TEST

The slump test is used to determine the workability or consistency of freshly mixed concrete. 
It is one of the most common field tests in concrete construction.

Apparatus:
- Slump cone (height: 300 mm)
- Tamping rod
- Base plate

Procedure:
1. The slump cone is placed on a level surface.
2. It is filled with fresh concrete in three equal layers.
3. Each layer is tamped 25 times using a tamping rod.
4. The cone is lifted vertically upward.
5. The subsidence of concrete is measured in millimeters.

Types of Slump:
- True slump
- Shear slump
- Collapse slump

Significance:
The slump value indicates the water-cement ratio and workability of the concrete mix.
